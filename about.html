3D PRINTED FIDGETS WITH A FOCUS ON FEEL.

FlowFidget is a small project built around one idea: make objects that are fun to hold, flip, spin, and slide while you think, play, or work.

How it started

It began with a single 3D printer and a simple spinner design. From there, the focus shifted to how each piece feels: the way a slider moves on its track, the way a ring rolls under your thumb, the way a cube sits in your palm.

What we care about

Motion, texture, and shape. Every design is printed, tested, adjusted, and re‑printed until it feels right. Some pieces are quiet and subtle, others are a bit more present, but all of them are made to be used, not just looked at.

Iterative design

We tweak tolerances, profiles, and textures based on how they feel in real use.

Material choices

PLA+, PETG, and flexible blends chosen for grip, smoothness, and durability.

Everyday use

Pieces that live on your desk, in your bag, or next to your console without getting in the way.

First prints

Simple spinners and sliders printed on a single machine, tuned by trial and error.

Texture experiments

Ridges, grooves, and curves tested to see what feels best to trace and tap.

Current lineup

A small collection of spinners, sliders, cubes, rings, and loops with different motion styles.
