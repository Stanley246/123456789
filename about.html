BUILT FOR RESTLESS HANDS. AND RESTLESS MINDS.

RogueFidget is a tiny, stubborn studio obsessed with 3D printed fidget gear that actually feels dangerous.

Why 3D printed?

Because it lets us move fast, break things, and iterate like maniacs. Every toy starts as a CAD sketch, gets stress‑tested on printers, then tuned by hand until it clicks, spins, or snaps just right.

The vibe

No soft pastels. No “mindfulness” branding. Just aggressive geometry, industrial textures, and unapologetic tactility. These are pocket‑sized machines for people who can’t sit still.

Small batch runs

We print in limited batches so we can tweak designs constantly and keep things fresh and weird.

Material choices

PLA+ and PETG for durability, with experimental blends for grip, texture, and that “just one more click” feel.

Designed to be used

Edges, ridges, channels, and bearings are all tuned for real‑world fidget abuse, not just shelf aesthetics.
